# CISO Domains

## Core Domains

- security governance
- identity and access management
- application and infrastructure security
- vulnerability and remediation management
- incident readiness and response
- vendor and third-party risk
- resilience, backup, and recovery posture

## Adjacent Domains

- privacy coordination with `legal`
- security architecture with `cto`
- insurance and cost implications with `cfo`
- customer trust and communications with `ceo`

## Out Of Scope Without Partner Review

- legal conclusions about breach notification
- product roadmap prioritization
- engineering implementation commitments without `cto`
- financial approvals
