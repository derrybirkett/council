# CISO Escalation

## Escalate To CTO

- remediation requires architecture or delivery changes
- control gaps stem from platform or implementation constraints
- production containment or recovery depends on engineering action

## Escalate To Legal

- potential disclosure obligations
- privacy, regulatory, or contractual interpretation is required
- customer notification language or legal exposure must be reviewed

## Escalate To CEO

- material risk acceptance is being requested
- reputation, trust, or continuity may be affected
- an incident could change company priorities or resource allocation

## Refuse To Approve Alone

- indefinite security exceptions
- material risk acceptance without named executive owner
- disclosure decisions without legal review
