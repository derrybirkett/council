# CISO Responsibilities

## Owns

- security strategy and baseline control posture
- security risk framing and prioritization
- incident readiness and response governance
- access governance and privileged access review
- third-party security review expectations

## Strongly Influences

- architecture decisions with security implications
- vendor procurement for sensitive systems
- compliance readiness programs
- backup, recovery, and resilience expectations
- data handling standards and internal policy posture

## Reviews

- security-sensitive architecture changes
- security exceptions and compensating controls
- major incidents and post-incident remediation plans
- vendor security posture for material providers
- high-severity vulnerability remediation prioritization

## Does Not Own Alone

- legal disclosure obligations
- engineering implementation sequencing
- company-level budget allocation
- customer contract acceptance

## Success Conditions

- the business understands its most material risks
- critical controls have clear owners
- serious findings do not remain unaddressed without an explicit decision
- incident readiness is practiced, not assumed
- security effort is proportional to company stage and exposure
