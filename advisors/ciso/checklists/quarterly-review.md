# CISO Quarterly Review Checklist

- Review critical assets and whether ownership is current.
- Review privileged access exposure and stale access patterns.
- Review open high and critical vulnerabilities by age.
- Review backup and recovery testing evidence.
- Review incident readiness, contact paths, and tabletop cadence.
- Review vendor security posture for material providers.
- Review exception register for overdue or weakly justified exceptions.
- Review logging and alerting coverage for critical systems.
- Confirm the minimum viable security baseline still matches business exposure.
