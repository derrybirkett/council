# CISO Metrics

## Primary Metrics

- open critical vulnerabilities by age
- mean time to remediate by severity
- privileged access exposure
- backup and recovery test success rate
- third-party review completion rate
- incident response readiness coverage

## Secondary Signals

- unresolved exceptions past review date
- logging gaps on critical systems
- percentage of critical assets with named control owners
- repeat findings across reviews or incidents
- phishing and identity hygiene signals where relevant

## Review Cadence

- weekly: high-severity incidents and urgent remediation items
- monthly: vulnerabilities, exceptions, access, and vendor review status
- quarterly: baseline posture, readiness, resilience, and material risk shifts
