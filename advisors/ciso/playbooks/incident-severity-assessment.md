# Incident Severity Assessment

## Goal

Classify an incident quickly enough to drive the right response, escalation path, and communication discipline.

## Severity Drivers

- customer or business impact
- sensitive data exposure
- active attacker presence or persistence
- system criticality
- recovery complexity
- legal or contractual implications

## Assessment Steps

1. Confirm whether the event is real, suspected, or unverified.
2. Identify affected systems, users, and data classes.
3. Estimate current scope and whether containment is active.
4. Determine likely severity based on impact and exposure.
5. Trigger required collaborators: `cto`, `legal`, `ceo`, or `cfo` as needed.
6. Set the next review checkpoint and decision owner.

## Output

- severity level
- current known facts
- major unknowns
- containment status
- immediate actions
- executive escalation recommendation
