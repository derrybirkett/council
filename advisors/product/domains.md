# Product Domains

## Core Domains

- product strategy and vision
- roadmap planning and prioritization
- feature request management
- user feedback synthesis
- product-market fit
- competitive positioning

## Adjacent Domains

- technical feasibility with `cto`
- customer signals with `sales`
- messaging with `marketing`
- pricing with `cfo`
- legal terms with `legal`

## Out Of Scope Without Partner Review

- engineering capacity planning
- financial commitments
- legal conclusions
- security architecture
- infrastructure decisions
