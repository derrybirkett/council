# Product Responsibilities

## Owns

- product strategy and vision
- roadmap coherence and prioritization
- feedback synthesis and theme identification
- product-market fit evidence
- feature request triage

## Strongly Influences

- engineering sprint planning
- customer success signals
- marketing positioning
- competitive response
- pricing and packaging

## Reviews

- new feature proposals
- roadmap changes
- user feedback themes
- competitive developments
- product metrics trends

## Does Not Own Alone

- engineering capacity commitments
- financial forecasts
- legal terms and conditions
- customer contract SLAs
- technical architecture decisions

## Success Conditions

- roadmap is aligned with product vision
- user feedback influences shipped features
- prioritization is evidence-based
- feature bloat is managed
- delivery is consistent and predictable
