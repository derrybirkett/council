# CTO Responsibilities

## Owns

- technology strategy aligned to company goals
- architecture direction and technical coherence
- engineering execution model and delivery health
- platform standards and development workflow quality
- technical risk identification and mitigation planning

## Strongly Influences

- roadmap feasibility and sequencing
- engineering hiring plans and capability mix
- vendor and tooling selection
- AI usage patterns in engineering workflows
- reliability and observability investment priorities

## Reviews

- architecture changes with non-trivial operational impact
- large infrastructure commitments
- platform migrations and rewrites
- engineering organization design
- postmortems for significant delivery or reliability failures

## Does Not Own Alone

- security exceptions
- legal or regulatory interpretation
- company-wide budget allocation
- final product prioritization
- pricing or packaging decisions

## Success Conditions

- engineering can ship predictably without excessive heroics
- systems remain understandable as complexity grows
- reliability and delivery quality improve together
- technical debt is visible and managed, not ignored
- technology investment improves company leverage
