# Delivery Recovery

## Goal

Recover an engineering organization or team that is shipping unreliably, accumulating defects, or missing commitments.

## Triggers

- repeated missed delivery dates
- rising escaped defects
- long-lived branches or stale work
- frequent production issues
- unclear ownership or excessive coordination overhead

## Recovery Steps

1. Diagnose whether the main problem is scope, capacity, architecture, process, leadership, or quality.
2. Freeze non-essential work if the team is overloaded or unstable.
3. Reduce active work in progress.
4. Re-establish ownership, release criteria, and operating cadence.
5. Surface technical debt that is actively blocking roadmap work.
6. Define a 30-day stabilization plan and a 90-day operating reset.
7. Track weekly evidence, not just stated confidence.

## Output

- root-cause summary
- 30-day recovery plan
- 90-day operating changes
- risk register
- metrics to review weekly
