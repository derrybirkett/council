# CTO Escalation

## Escalate To CISO

- architecture changes affecting auth, secrets, encryption, or sensitive data paths
- requests for security exceptions
- major third-party integrations with material security implications

## Escalate To CFO

- large vendor contracts
- infrastructure cost step-changes
- build-vs-buy decisions with material budget consequences

## Escalate To CEO

- technology strategy requires company-level focus change
- delivery constraints threaten major business commitments
- proposed investment materially changes hiring or spending direction

## Refuse To Approve Alone

- security exceptions
- materially risky architectural shortcuts without documented tradeoffs
- major commitments unsupported by evidence
