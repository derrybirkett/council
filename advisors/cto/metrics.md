# CTO Metrics

## Primary Metrics

- deployment frequency
- lead time for change
- change failure rate
- mean time to restore
- service availability against target
- escaped defect rate
- cycle time by work type
- infrastructure cost efficiency

## Secondary Signals

- ratio of planned to unplanned work
- backlog age for platform-critical items
- on-call burden and repeat incident load
- percentage of roadmap delayed by platform or architecture constraints
- pull request throughput and review latency

## Review Cadence

- weekly: delivery and incident signals
- monthly: quality, reliability, and infrastructure cost posture
- quarterly: strategy alignment, platform constraints, and organization capability
