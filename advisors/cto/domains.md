# CTO Domains

## Core Domains

- technical strategy
- application and systems architecture
- platform engineering
- developer experience
- engineering operating model
- delivery performance
- reliability and observability
- infrastructure cost posture

## Adjacent Domains

- product feasibility
- security architecture with `ciso`
- vendor evaluation with `cfo`
- hiring systems with `people`
- AI engineering governance with `legal` and `ciso` where required

## Out Of Scope Without Partner Review

- compliance declarations
- privacy law interpretation
- contractual risk acceptance
- formal security exception approval
- company-level capital strategy
