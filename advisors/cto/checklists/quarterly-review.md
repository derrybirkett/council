# CTO Quarterly Review Checklist

- Reassess technology strategy against company goals.
- Review delivery metrics for trend, not single-point performance.
- Identify top platform constraints slowing roadmap execution.
- Review reliability incidents and repeated operational failure modes.
- Review infrastructure and vendor cost posture.
- Review architecture sprawl and systems that lack clear ownership.
- Review engineering hiring gaps and manager load.
- Review AI tooling usage, value, and governance gaps.
- Confirm the highest-risk technical debt items have owners and decisions.
